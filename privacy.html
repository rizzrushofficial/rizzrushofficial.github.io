Rizz Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Developer: Rizz Rush · Contact: rizzrushofficial@gmail.com

The short version
  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or photos.
  • Your game progress is stored on your own iPhone. We don't run servers that collect it.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may collect device and ad data. You choose whether to allow tracking.
  • Payments go through Apple. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Gameplay clips stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

This policy explains what information the Rizz Rush mobile game ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app stores the following on your iPhone only:

  • Coins, high score, unlocked characters and maps, selected character and map, play time, tutorial and daily-reward progress, and a list of purchase transactions already credited.
  • Settings such as sound, music, voice lines, haptics and clip recording.
  • Your birth month and year, asked once on first launch. It is used only to choose the right ad settings for your age (see section 7) and is never sent to us or to advertisers.

Game progress is kept in the iPhone Keychain with a tamper-protection seal, which prevents cheating and lets your progress come back if you reinstall the app. Because of this, deleting the app does not by itself erase your progress. To erase it completely, use Settings → Delete my progress in the app. Like other Keychain items, it may be included in your encrypted iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple manages. We do not upload this information to our own servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and protect ads (for example, to limit how often you see an ad and to prevent fraud), Google may collect:

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks for permission to track. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are shown a consent form before personalized ads, and you can change your choice any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off forced ads. Optional "watch an ad" rewards are still available if you want them.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-app purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, card or payment details. The app receives a transaction record signed by Apple (product, date and a transaction ID), checks it on your device, and uses it to unlock what you bought, to make sure each purchase is credited once, and to restore purchases. If Apple refunds or revokes a purchase, the related item is removed from the game.

4. Gameplay clips and sharing

If "Record clips for sharing" is turned on in Settings, the app uses Apple's ReplayKit to record the game screen and game sound while you play, so it can save your last 15 seconds when a run ends. iOS asks your permission before recording. The app never records your microphone or camera and does not capture anything outside the app.

Recordings are processed on your device. Temporary recordings are deleted automatically; a saved clip only leaves your phone if you choose to share it (for example to TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, YouTube or Messages). Once shared, it is handled by that service's own privacy policy. You can turn clip recording off at any time.

5. Voice lines and other on-device features

Spoken voice lines are generated on your device by Apple's built-in text-to-speech. Haptics, music and sound effects run entirely on your device. None of this sends data to us.

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders (for example, that your daily reward is ready) directly on your iPhone. They are created from your game progress on the device; no data is sent to us or to a notification server. You can turn them off in the app under Settings → Reminders, or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple ID. You can turn Game Center off in iPhone Settings → Game Center.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't require or offer accounts or logins.
  • We don't collect your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We don't use third-party analytics in the app, and we don't sell your personal information.

7. Children and age-based settings

Rizz Rush is a general-audience game. When the app is first opened, it asks for your birth month and year. This answer stays on your device and only decides how ads work:

  • Under 13: the app does not ask for permission to track and tells Google AdMob to treat ad requests as directed to children. This means no personalized or interest-based ads, and ads limited to content suitable for general audiences, as described in Google's policies for child-directed treatment.
  • 13 to 15: the app tells Google AdMob to treat ad requests as coming from users under the age of consent, so ads are not personalized in places where parental consent would be required (such as the EEA and UK).
  • 16 and over: standard ad settings apply, including the tracking permission and, where required, the consent form described in section 2.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it. Purchases by minors are subject to Apple's parental controls (such as Ask to Buy).

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.

  • Your game data stays on your device. You can erase it at any time in the app with Settings → Delete my progress (purchases made through Apple can still be restored afterwards).
  • To opt out of targeted advertising, choose "Ask App Not to Track" (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) and, in the EEA/UK, use Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • For data collected by Google for ads, you can also use Google's controls at adssettings.google.com.
  • For any other request, email us. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases we rely on are: performance of our agreement with you (running the game and delivering purchases), your consent (personalized advertising), and our legitimate interests (preventing cheating and fraud, keeping the game working).

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including storing game progress in the iPhone Keychain with tamper protection and only accepting purchase records signed by Apple. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

10. Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you erase it with Settings → Delete my progress. Temporary gameplay recordings are deleted automatically after a clip is created or when recording stops. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will also let you know in the app or in the App Store release notes.
