Common questions

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes and swipe up to jump. Dodge red obstacles, jump over yellow ones. Your aura bar is your life: hits take aura, and getting hit with no aura left ends the run. In the app, open Settings → How to play for the full guide.

I bought something but didn't get it

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Coin packs are delivered once, right after the purchase. If something is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase. For refunds, Apple handles all payments: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

No. Your progress is saved securely on your iPhone and comes back if you reinstall. To erase it completely, use Settings → Delete my progress in the app.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the Store. Ads between runs go away. Optional "watch an ad" rewards (revive, 2x coins) stay available if you want them. The Galaxy Brain character removes all ads completely.

No sound or music?

Check that Sound and Music are on in the game's Settings and that your iPhone volume is up. If it's still quiet, close the app fully (swipe it away) and open it again.

How do I change ad or tracking choices?

You can turn tracking off any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the EU/UK can also use Privacy choices in the game's Settings.

Parents

Rizz Rush asks for the player's age on first launch. Players under 13 only get non-personalized, general-audience ads and are never asked for tracking permission. Purchases can be blocked with Screen Time or Ask to Buy. Questions? Email rizzrushofficial@gmail.com.
